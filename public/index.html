Accuracy & data

AR values are accepted only from an exact ISBN search in AR Bookfinder. The app does not guess AR values from a similar title or another edition.

“No AR quiz for this ISBN” means no exact AR result was found for the scanned ISBN; it does not claim that every edition of that title lacks an AR quiz. Title/author fallback metadata may come from Open Library when no AR result is available.
